## Why this grid

The frozen Conversion Lab showed:
- 0.5R and 1.0R symmetric barriers destroy expectancy
- 1.5R remains weak
- 2.0R/H60 is the best symmetric barrier result
- MFE/MAE extrema continue developing through roughly 30-60 minutes
- the unresolved question is execution, not entry selection

Therefore the grid concentrates on:
- H30/H60 time exits
- 2.0R to 2.5R TP/SL brackets
- break-even activation after +1R or +1.5R
- 50% partial realization after +1R or +1.5R with the runner protected at BE

No session, side, skew, VOV, RSI, ATR, weekday, or other entry filter is tested.

## Intrabar conventions

All ambiguity is resolved conservatively:
- TP and SL touched in the same M1: SL first
- BE trigger activates only from the next M1
- after BE is active, BE and TP touched in same M1: BE first
- partial trigger and initial SL in same M1: full initial SL first
- runner TP and initial SL in same M1 before partial protection: full initial SL first
- partial level and runner TP in the same M1 without SL: both fills are accepted because reaching runner TP necessarily traverses the partial level
- after partial/BE, runner TP and BE touched in same M1: BE first

## Costs

Legacy Atlas stress semantics:
- gross R is calculated from exact execution
- 0.10 or 0.20 absolute XAUUSD price units are subtracted once from total-position PnL, then divided by entry risk
- for partial exits, cost is applied to total unit exposure, not double-counted by leg

## Selection rule

If several candidates pass all strict conditions, select exactly one for possible final 2026 validation by:
1. highest minimum yearly MeanR at .10
2. then highest full-sample MeanR at .20
3. then lowest full-sample max drawdown at .10
4. then deterministic candidate id
