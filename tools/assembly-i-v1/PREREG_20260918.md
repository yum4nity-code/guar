## Strategies

### AS1_SKEW_KURT
- Prior 24 completed M5 returns, sampled hourly.
- Realized skew: abs(skew) >= 1.0.
- Excess kurtosis >= 3.0.
- Direction: opposite skew sign.
- Same-event control: with skew sign.
- Primary H60; H30/H120 descriptive only.
- Components: A4_02 + A4_04.

### AS2_SKEW_VOV
- Prior 24 completed M5 returns, sampled hourly.
- Realized skew: abs(skew) >= 1.0.
- Four six-return realized-variance blocks; coefficient of variation >= 0.75.
- Direction: opposite skew sign.
- Same-event control: with skew sign.
- Primary H60; H30/H120 descriptive only.
- Components: A4_02 + A4_03.

### AS3_TAIL_KURT
- Prior 24 completed M5 returns, sampled hourly.
- Robust scale = 1.4826 * MAD around median.
- Tails beyond median +/- 1.5 robust-scale.
- Require >=4 total tails and abs(up-tail count - down-tail count) >=3.
- Excess kurtosis >= 3.0.
- Direction: opposite dominant tail side.
- Same-event control: with dominant tail side.
- Primary H60; H30/H120 descriptive only.
- Components: A5_03 + A4_04.

## Cost semantics

For comparability with Atlas IV/V, directional stress keeps the legacy Atlas convention: subtract 0.10 or 0.20 absolute XAUUSD price units from PnL before normalization by 1R, where 1R = 1.5 * Wilder ATR14 M5.

## External OOS gate

A strategy passes only if all are true:
- N >= 60
- .10 PF >= 1.10
- .10 MeanR > 0
- .20 PF >= 1.05
- .20 MeanR > 0
- >=2 of 3 calendar years have positive .10 MeanR
- daily-block bootstrap signal CI95 lower > 0
- MeanR excluding top 1% winners > 0
- signal-minus-opposite-control daily delta > 0
- daily delta CI95 lower > 0
- exact same-event matched-control delta > 0
- joint BH q <= 0.10 across the three primary tests

No secondary horizon can rescue a failed H60 result. No post-hoc threshold, session, side, horizon, or exit tuning is authorized on 2023-2025.
