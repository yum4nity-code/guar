## Directional families

### A5_01_RETURN_AUTOCORR_REGIME
Hourly, prior 24 completed M5 returns. Compute lag-1 Pearson autocorrelation. If |rho| >= 0.25, follow the latest return when rho > 0 and fade it when rho < 0. Same-event opposite-direction control. Primary H60; secondary H30/H120.

### A5_02_ROBUST_LOCATION_SHIFT
Hourly, prior 24 completed M5 returns. Robust location score = |median return| / (1.4826 * MAD). Signal if score >= 0.35; follow median sign. Same-event opposite-direction control. Primary H60; secondary H30/H120.

### A5_03_TAIL_COUNT_EXHAUSTION
Hourly, prior 24 completed M5 returns. Robust tails are returns beyond median +/- 1.5*(1.4826*MAD). Require at least 4 total tails and an absolute positive-vs-negative tail-count imbalance >= 3. Trade opposite dominant tail side. Same-event continuation control. Primary H60; secondary H30/H120.

## Amplitude-regime families

### A5_04_PERMUTATION_ENTROPY
Hourly, order-3 normalized permutation entropy of prior 24 completed M5 returns. Signal <= 0.75; control >= 0.95. Primary amplitude outcome is max(MFE_R, MAE_R) at H60; H120 descriptive.

### A5_05_VOL_CLUSTER_AUTOCORR
Hourly, lag-1 Pearson autocorrelation of squared prior 24 completed M5 returns. Signal >= 0.25; control |rho| <= 0.05. Primary amplitude outcome H60; H120 descriptive.

### A5_06_RANGE_RETENTION
Hourly, prior 12 completed M5 bars. Retention = sum absolute close-to-close travel / sum intrabar ranges. Signal <= 0.35; control >= 0.65. Primary amplitude outcome H60; H120 descriptive.

## Gates

Directional and amplitude gates are the same preregistered gates used by Atlas IV, including 0.10 / 0.20 cost stress for directional families, internal 2021-2022 holdout, leave-one-year-out tests, daily bootstrap, matched controls, top-1%-winner concentration check, and joint Benjamini-Hochberg correction across all six primary family tests.

No 2023-2025 opening is authorized merely by a cheap gate or amplitude phenomenon gate. A directional family must reach the strict freeze candidate gate before an external OOS opening is considered.
